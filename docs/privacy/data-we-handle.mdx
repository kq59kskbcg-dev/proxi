## What is stored

| Data | Where | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Your account (sign-in) | Clerk | Handles sign-in and sessions. |
| Your chats | Postgres database (Neon) | Stored **after masking**, and tied to your account ID. Only you can list, open or delete your chats. |
| Message traces | Langfuse (EU) | Masked message text, the model used, token counts and your account ID, used to monitor quality and errors. |
| Feedback | Langfuse | Your thumbs up or down on a reply. |

## What is not stored

- **Attached files.** They are used for a single request and never saved. Saved chats only remember that an image, PDF or file was attached.
- **Unmasked sensitive data.** Card numbers, IBANs, emails and phone numbers are masked before they reach the model, the logs or the database. See [Sensitive data](/privacy/sensitive-data).

## Who processes your messages

Your masked messages are sent to Anthropic's Claude API to produce replies. Sign-in is handled by Clerk, chats by Neon, and monitoring by Langfuse.

## Deleting your chats

Open a chat in the sidebar and choose **Delete chat**. This removes the chat and all its messages from the database. Chats are otherwise kept until you delete them.

<Note>
Traces and feedback in Langfuse are kept separately from your saved chats. Deleting a chat does not remove them.
</Note>
