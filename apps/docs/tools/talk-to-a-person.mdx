---
title: "Talk to a person"
description: "Send us a message from the chat when you need a person."
---

Ask Proxi to talk to a person and it offers to pass a message on to us. A card under the reply shows the short summary it wrote of what you need. Add your email address and choose **Send**, or choose **Not now** to skip it. Nothing is sent until you choose Send.

## What we receive

We get the summary by email, with your address so we can reply. The summary is redacted like your messages. Your address is used only for that email and isn't saved with your chat.

## Saved and incognito chats

In a saved chat, the card stays with the reply and shows that it was sent. You can also send a message from an incognito chat, which isn't saved.

## Limits

- You can only send a few messages in a short time.
- If sending isn't available, the card tells you and gives you an email address to write to instead.
