---
title: "Search your chats"
description: "Proxi can look up things you talked about in your earlier chats."
---

Proxi can look through your saved chats when you refer to something you talked about before, like "the hotel we picked last week".

**What it searches.** Only your own saved chats, by title and message text. It doesn't search the chat you're in, and it can't see anyone else's chats. Chats you deleted, or that were removed by automatic deletion, are gone and can't be found.

**Incognito.** In an incognito chat, Proxi doesn't search your past chats, and incognito chats are never saved, so they can't be found later.

**See what it used.** The reply's steps show what Proxi searched for. Chats it used are listed under Sources with their title and date. Pick one to open it.

**Limits.** Proxi finds up to 5 chats per search and reads short passages from them, not whole chats. Chats are stored with card numbers, IBANs, emails and phone numbers masked, so those details can't be found.
