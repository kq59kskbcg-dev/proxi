---

## Delete a chat

Open the chat, choose the chat title at the top, then **Delete chat**. If the chat was [shared](/chats/share), its link stops working.

## Automatic deletion

Chats are deleted after 30 days without activity. To change this, go to **Settings**, then **Delete chats after**, and choose 7, 30 or 90 days.

## What deleting covers

Deleting a chat removes it from your history. See the [privacy policy](/privacy-policy#how-long-we-keep-it) for how long other data is kept.
