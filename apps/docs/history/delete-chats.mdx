---
title: "Delete your chats"
description: "Delete one chat or all of them, or let Proxi delete chats after 7, 30 or 90 days."
---

## Delete a chat

Open the chat, choose the chat title at the top, then **Delete chat**. If the chat was [shared](/chats/share), its link stops working.

## Delete all chats

Go to **Settings**, then **Delete all chats**, and confirm. This deletes every chat in your history and their monitoring traces. Shared links stop working. Your memories, settings and privacy counts are kept. You can't undo this.

## Automatic deletion

Chats are deleted after 30 days without activity. To change this, go to **Settings**, then **Delete chats after**, and choose 7, 30 or 90 days.

## What deleting covers

Deleting a chat removes it from your history, and deletes its monitoring trace. To delete everything at once, [delete your account](/settings/delete-account).
