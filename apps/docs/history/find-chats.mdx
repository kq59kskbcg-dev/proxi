## Recents

The sidebar lists your 10 most recent chats under **Recents**. Pick one to open it.

## View all and search

To see the rest, choose **View all**, use **Search chats** at the top of the sidebar, or press Ctrl K (⌘K on a Mac).

With nothing typed, search lists all your chats, newest first. Type to find chats by title, then pick one to open it. Press Escape to close search.

## What isn't listed

[Incognito chats](/chats/incognito) aren't saved, so they don't show up in the sidebar or in search. Chats removed by [automatic deletion](/history/delete-chats#automatic-deletion) are gone.
