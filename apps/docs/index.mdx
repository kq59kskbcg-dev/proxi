Proxi stands between you and the AI. It works like any AI assistant: ask questions, draft text, or attach PDFs, images and notes. But every message passes through Proxi first. Your identity stays with Proxi, sensitive details like card numbers and emails are redacted before anything reaches the model, and nothing you share is used to train AI. You get the power of Claude without handing over your data.
