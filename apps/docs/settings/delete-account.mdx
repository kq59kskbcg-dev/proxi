---
title: "Delete your account"
description: "Delete your account and everything Proxi stores about you."
---

To delete your account, go to **Settings**, then the **General** tab, find **Delete account** under **Account**, choose **Delete**, then confirm.

## What is deleted

- Your chats, including the monitoring traces of those chats.
- Your [memories](/settings/memory) and [settings](/settings/preferences), including your [custom instructions](/settings/custom-instructions).
- The counts on your [Privacy page](/your-privacy).
- Your sign-in account.

[Shared](/chats/share) links stop working, because the chats behind them are deleted.

You can't undo this. After it finishes, you are signed out. To use Proxi again, create a new account.

## Other ways to delete

To remove only some of your data, [delete a chat](/history/delete-chats) or a [memory](/settings/memory). You can also email us to exercise your rights, as described in the [privacy policy](/privacy-policy).
