---
title: "Memory"
description: "Short facts Proxi remembers across chats, and how to delete them."
---

Proxi can remember short facts about you across chats, such as how you like replies written or a project you're working on. Ask it directly, for example "remember that I use metric units", or it may suggest one when you share a lasting preference. Nothing is saved until you approve it.

## Approve a memory

When Proxi wants to remember something, a **Save to memory** card appears under its reply with the exact text. You can change the text first.

- Choose **Save** to save it. The card changes to **Saved to memory**.
- Choose **Not now** to skip it. The card changes to **Not saved to memory**, and Proxi won't suggest it again in that chat.
- If you send another message without choosing **Save**, it counts as **Not now**. Typing "yes" doesn't save it.

The card keeps its state when you come back to the chat.

## Manage memories

Your saved memories are listed in **Settings**, under **Memory**. Use **Delete memory** to remove any of them.

## Limits and privacy

- Memories are redacted like messages, so names, addresses, card numbers, IBANs, emails and phone numbers are removed before they're saved, including text you change in the card.
- You can keep up to 50 memories. They stay until you delete them.
- [Incognito chats](/chats/incognito) never save to memory and don't use your saved memories.
