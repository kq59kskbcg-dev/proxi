title: "Settings overview"
description: "Theme, chat deletion, memory, shared links, custom instructions, account and legal pages."
---

Open **Settings** from your profile menu at the bottom of the sidebar. Select the X at the top right to close it.

| Setting | What it does |
|---|---|
| Theme | Match your device, or always use light or dark |
| Delete chats after | Choose 7, 30 or 90 days. See [Delete your chats](/history/delete-chats) |
| Delete all chats | Delete every chat at once. See [Delete your chats](/history/delete-chats) |
| [Memory](/settings/memory) | See and delete what Proxi remembers |
| [Shared links](/chats/share#see-all-your-shared-links) | See every chat you're sharing, and stop sharing any of them |
| [Custom instructions](/settings/custom-instructions) | Tell Proxi how to respond |
| [Account](/settings/delete-account) | Delete your account and its data |

The [privacy policy](/privacy-policy) and [terms of service](/terms) are linked at the bottom of Settings.
