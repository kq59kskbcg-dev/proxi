title: "Settings"
description: "Theme, chat deletion, memory, custom instructions and legal pages."
---

Open **Settings** from the sidebar.

| Setting | What it does |
|---|---|
| Theme | Match your device, or always use light or dark |
| Delete chats after | Choose 7, 30 or 90 days. See [Delete your chats](/history/delete-chats) |
| [Memory](/settings/memory) | See and delete what Proxi remembers |
| [Custom instructions](/settings/custom-instructions) | Tell Proxi how to respond |

The [privacy policy](/privacy-policy) and [terms of service](/terms) are linked at the bottom of Settings.
