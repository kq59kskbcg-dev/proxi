---

Open **Settings** from your profile menu at the bottom of the sidebar. It has five tabs: General, Memory, Shared links, Tools and History. On a phone they're across the top; on a computer they're listed on the left. Select the X at the top right to close Settings.

| Tab | Setting | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| General | Theme | Match your device, or always use light or dark |
| General | [Custom instructions](/settings/custom-instructions) | Tell Proxi how to respond |
| General | Log out | Log out of Proxi |
| General | [Delete account](/settings/delete-account) | Delete your account and its data |
| Memory | [Memory](/settings/memory) | See and delete what Proxi remembers |
| Shared links | [Shared links](/chats/share#see-all-your-shared-links) | See every chat you're sharing, and stop sharing any of them |
| Tools | [Tools](/tools/overview#turn-tools-off) | Choose which tools Proxi can use |
| History | Delete chats after | Choose 7, 30 or 90 days. See [Delete your chats](/history/delete-chats) |
| History | Delete all chats | Delete every chat at once. See [Delete your chats](/history/delete-chats) |

The [privacy policy](/privacy-policy) and [terms of service](/terms) are linked at the bottom of the General tab.
