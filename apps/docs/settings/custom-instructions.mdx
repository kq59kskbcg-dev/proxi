---
title: "Custom instructions"
description: "Tell Proxi about yourself and how you'd like it to respond."
---

In **Settings**, under **Custom instructions**, tell Proxi about yourself and how you'd like it to respond, for example "I'm a product designer. Keep answers short and use bullet points."

- Up to 1,500 characters.
- They apply to every chat, including [incognito chats](/chats/incognito), from your next message.
- Save an empty box to remove them.

For short facts Proxi should recall on its own, see [Memory](/settings/memory).
