title: "Your privacy page"
description: "See how many personal details Proxi removed from your messages."
---

Open **Privacy** in the menu, under **New chat**, to see how many personal details Proxi removed from your messages before sending them to Claude.

## What it shows

- The total for this month or all time
- A count for each type: emails, phone numbers, card numbers and IBANs
- A chart by week (this month) or by month (all time)

## What's counted

- Only totals by type and day are saved, never the details themselves.
- Counting started in November 2026. Earlier messages aren't counted.
- Incognito chats aren't counted.
- Deleting a chat doesn't change the counts. Deleting your account deletes them.
