_Last updated: 27 October 2026_

Proxi is a personal project run by Lisandro Vaccaro, based in the Netherlands. This policy explains what personal data Proxi handles, why, and what rights you have. For any privacy question or request, email [dealing-lignite-1z@icloud.com](mailto:dealing-lignite-1z@icloud.com).

## Who is responsible

Lisandro Vaccaro is the data controller for personal data processed through Proxi.

## What we collect

| Data | Examples | Where it comes from |
|---|---|---|
| Account data | Email address, name, profile picture | You, when you create an account |
| Chat content | Your messages and Proxi's replies, after redaction, and web search queries Proxi makes for you | You, when you use the chat |
| Attachments | Files you attach to a message | You. Files are processed for that one request and not stored |
| Usage and monitoring data | Redacted messages, the model used, token counts, errors. Monitoring traces don't include your account ID | Generated when you use Proxi, except in temporary chats |
| Feedback | Thumbs up or down on a reply | You |
| Technical data | IP address, browser details, request logs | Your device, when you visit |

Before a message is sent anywhere, Proxi automatically removes card numbers, IBANs, email addresses and phone numbers from it. Other personal details you type, such as names or addresses, are not removed, so please avoid sharing information you don't need to.

## Why we use it

| Purpose | Legal basis |
|---|---|
| Creating your account and letting you sign in | Performance of a contract (providing the service you signed up for) |
| Generating replies and saving your chats | Performance of a contract |
| Keeping Proxi secure, preventing abuse and rate limiting | Legitimate interests (security of the service) |
| Monitoring errors and improving answer quality | Legitimate interests (operating and improving the service) |
| Recording your feedback | Legitimate interests (improving the service) |

We don't sell your data, use it for advertising, or make decisions about you that have legal or similarly significant effects.

## Who we share it with

We use these service providers (subprocessors) to run Proxi. They process data only on our behalf.

| Provider | Purpose | Location |
|---|---|---|
| Anthropic | Generates replies with Claude, and runs web searches (through its own search provider) | United States |
| Clerk | Sign-in and account management | United States |
| Neon | Database for saved chats | Cloud hosted |
| Langfuse | Monitoring, tracing and feedback | European Union |
| Vercel | Hosting | Functions run in Frankfurt, EU. Vercel is a US company |

Under its commercial terms, Anthropic does not use data sent through its API to train its models.

## International transfers

Some providers are based in the United States, so your data may be transferred outside the EU and UK. Where this happens, we rely on the safeguards each provider offers, such as the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework or the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses.

## How long we keep it

| Data | Retention |
|---|---|
| Account data | Until you delete your account |
| Chats | Deleted automatically after 30 days without activity, or after 7 or 90 days if you choose that in Settings. Sooner if you delete the chat or your account |
| Attachments | Not stored. Used for one request only |
| Monitoring traces and feedback | As long as needed to operate and debug Proxi, and deleted on request |
| Temporary chats | Not stored and not traced |
| Technical logs | According to our hosting provider's standard log retention |

## Your rights

Depending on where you live, you have the right to:

- Access the personal data we hold about you
- Correct inaccurate data
- Delete your data
- Restrict or object to how we use it
- Receive your data in a portable format
- Complain to a data protection authority, such as the Dutch Autoriteit Persoonsgegevens

To use any of these rights, email [dealing-lignite-1z@icloud.com](mailto:dealing-lignite-1z@icloud.com). You can delete individual chats yourself at any time from the sidebar. We'll reply within one month.

## Security

Data is encrypted in transit and at rest, sensitive data is redacted before it reaches the AI, and attachments are never stored. See [Security](/security) for details.

## Children

Proxi is not intended for anyone under 16, and we don't knowingly collect their data.

## Changes

If we change this policy, we'll update the date at the top. For significant changes, we'll let you know in the app.
