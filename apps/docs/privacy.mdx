## Limitations

- **Redaction covers structured data only.** Proxi redacts cards, IBANs, emails and phone numbers before anything leaves its servers, but not names or addresses. Pattern matching runs at no extra cost. A company could add a small self-hosted model to catch the rest.
- **Observability runs on Langfuse Cloud.** Redacted conversations are stored by Langfuse in the EU. The cloud version is free and needs no maintenance. In a company, Langfuse could be self-hosted so the data never leaves its infrastructure.

## Subprocessors

These providers process data on Proxi's behalf.

| Provider | Purpose | Data processed |
|---|---|---|
| Anthropic | Generates replies with Claude, and runs web searches | Redacted messages, attachments and search queries |
| Clerk | Sign-in and sessions | Account email and name |
| Neon | Database for saved chats | Redacted chats |
| Langfuse (EU) | Monitoring, tracing and feedback | Redacted messages, usage and feedback |
| Vercel | Hosting (functions in Frankfurt, EU) | App requests |
