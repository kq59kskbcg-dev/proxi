## Subprocessors

These providers process data on Proxi's behalf.

| Provider | Purpose | Data processed | Location |
|---|---|---|---|
| Anthropic | Generates replies with Claude, and runs web searches | Redacted messages, attachments and search queries | US |
| Clerk | Sign-in and sessions | Account email and name | US |
| Neon | Database for saved chats | Redacted chats | EU (Frankfurt) |
| Langfuse | Monitoring, tracing and feedback | Redacted messages, usage and feedback | EU |
| Vercel | Hosting | App requests | EU (Frankfurt) |

## Limitations

Proxi is a personal project. These are known tradeoffs made to keep it free to run.

- **Redaction covers structured data only.** Proxi uses pattern matching to redact cards, IBANs, emails and phone numbers, but can't catch names or addresses. A company could add a small self-hosted model for those.
- **Observability runs on Langfuse Cloud.** Redacted conversations are stored by Langfuse in the EU. A company could self-host it to keep that data in its own infrastructure.
