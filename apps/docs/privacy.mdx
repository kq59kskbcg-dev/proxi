title: "Privacy"
description: "How Proxi handles your data. Greyed out items are planned."
---

<Card title="Sensitive data masking" icon="eye-slash">
  Card numbers, IBANs, emails and phone numbers are masked before reaching the model, the logs or the database.
</Card>

<Card title="Attachments are never stored" icon="paperclip">
  Files are used for one request only. Text PDFs containing card numbers or IBANs are refused. Emails and phone numbers inside PDFs are not masked.
</Card>

<div style={{opacity: 0.5}}>
  <Card title="Image scanning" icon="image">
    <Badge color="gray" shape="pill">Planned</Badge>
    <br />
    Images can't be scanned for sensitive data yet, so Proxi warns you before you attach one.
  </Card>

  <Card title="Retention policy" icon="clock">
    <Badge color="gray" shape="pill">Planned</Badge>
    <br />
    Admins set how long chats and traces are kept. Data is deleted automatically when the period ends, legal holds pause deletion for audits, and every deletion is logged.
  </Card>
</div>
