## What is Proxi

Proxi is an AI chat assistant with built-in privacy. It sits between you and the AI model, Claude, and removes who you are before your message is sent. Your name, email, account and IP address are never shared with the model provider, and personal details in your messages, such as names, addresses, card numbers, IBANs, emails and phone numbers, are detected and replaced before they reach the model. You get the answers of a frontier model with much greater privacy. Proxi is built and maintained by one person.

## How it's built

<Card title="Privacy and security first" icon="shield-check">
  Sensitive data is kept out of the conversation from the start.

  - Privacy by default, with sensitive data redacted before the AI.
  - Data minimization, with no files stored and only redacted history.
  - Least privilege, with read-only tools and approved sources only.
  - Defense in depth, from encryption in transit and at rest to rate limits.
</Card>

<Card title="MCP tool integration" icon="plug">
  Proxi discovers and calls tools through its own MCP server, instead of having them hard-coded.

  - Tools for searching the web, searching the Proxi docs and checking service status
  - Tools are marked read-only, so the agent can't change anything
  - Up to five tool rounds per reply, with clear errors when a tool fails
</Card>

<Card title="RAG with citations" icon="book-open">
  Answers about Proxi come from retrieved documentation, with links to the sources used.

  - Relevant passages retrieved from this help center
  - Every answer cites the pages it drew on
</Card>

<Card title="Evaluation and observability" icon="chart-line">
  Replies are traced in Langfuse, so behavior can be inspected and improved. Incognito chats are not traced.

  - Traces of each reply, including every tool call, with redacted messages and no name or account details
  - The system prompt is managed and versioned in Langfuse
  - User feedback recorded as scores on each reply
</Card>
