## Search your chats

The sidebar lists your 10 most recent chats under **Recents**. To see the rest, choose **View all**, use **Search chats** at the top of the sidebar, or press Ctrl K (⌘K on a Mac). With nothing typed, search lists all your chats, newest first. Type to find chats by title, then pick one to open it. Incognito chats aren't saved, so they don't show up in search.

## Choose a model

Proxi uses **Auto** unless you pick a model. Auto checks how hard each message is and answers with the model that fits: Haiku for quick questions, Sonnet for most everyday requests, and Opus for work that needs careful reasoning. To choose one yourself, use the model menu next to the send button. Each reply's steps show which model answered.

## Incognito chats

Turn on incognito with the dashed chat bubble at the top right of a new chat. Incognito chats aren't saved to your history, aren't traced for monitoring, don't save to memory and aren't used to train AI models. An incognito chat isn't listed with your chats, disappears when you reload the page, and can't be shared. See [how incognito compares](/privacy#incognito-chats) with other AI apps.

## Memory

Proxi can remember short facts about you across chats, such as how you like replies written or a project you're working on. Ask it directly, for example "remember that I use metric units", and it saves the fact and tells you.

- Your saved memories are listed in **Settings**, under **Memory**, where you can delete any of them.
- Memories are redacted like messages, so card numbers, IBANs, emails and phone numbers are never saved.
- You can keep up to 50 memories. They stay until you delete them.
- Incognito chats never save to memory and don't use your saved memories.

## Custom instructions

In **Settings**, under **Custom instructions**, tell Proxi about yourself and how you'd like it to respond, in up to 1,500 characters. They apply to every chat, including incognito chats, from your next message. Save an empty box to remove them.

## Share a chat

Use **Share** at the top of a chat to turn on a public, read-only link and copy it.

- Anyone with the link can read the chat, as Proxi saw it: redacted, with no name or account details. Attachments show only as "PDF attached".
- New messages in the chat also become visible through the link.
- **Stop sharing**, or deleting the chat, turns the link off.
- Shared chats are kept out of search engines.

## How long chats are kept

Chats are deleted after 30 days without activity. You can choose 7, 30 or 90 days in **Settings**, under **Delete chats after**. You can also delete a chat yourself at any time.
