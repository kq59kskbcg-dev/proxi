<Update label="October 2026" description="Incognito chats, memory and more">
  - **Incognito chats.** Temporary chats are now called incognito chats, with a new icon and a link to what they keep out.
  - **Memory.** Ask Proxi to remember something and it will use it in future chats. Saved memories are listed in Settings, where you can delete them.
  - **Custom instructions.** Tell Proxi about yourself and how you'd like it to respond, in Settings.
  - **Share a chat.** Turn on a public, read-only link for any chat, and turn it off again at any time.
  - **Help center.** New pages on [using Proxi](/using-proxi), [how Proxi is tested](/how-proxi-is-tested) and [how Proxi's privacy compares](/privacy#how-proxi-compares) with other AI apps.
</Update>
