<Update label="October 29, 2026" description="Incognito chats, memory, custom instructions, search, Auto model and a shorter sidebar">
  - **Incognito chats.** Temporary chats are now called incognito chats, with a new icon and a link to what they keep out.
  - **Memory.** Ask Proxi to remember something and it will use it in future chats. Saved memories are listed in Settings, where you can delete them.
  - **Custom instructions.** Tell Proxi about yourself and how you'd like it to respond, in Settings.
  - **Search your chats.** A search field at the top of the sidebar, or Ctrl K (⌘K on a Mac), finds chats by title.
  - **Steadier sidebar button.** The button that opens and closes the sidebar now stays in the same place.
  - **Privacy policy and terms** moved from the account menu to Settings.
  - **Auto model.** Auto is the new default: it picks Haiku, Sonnet or Opus for each message, depending on how hard it is. You can still choose a model yourself. See [Choose a model](/using-proxi#choose-a-model).
  - **Calmer look.** Incognito chats and the model menu use quieter colors, and Settings is simpler.
  - **A shorter sidebar.** Your 10 most recent chats are listed under Recents, with a **View all** link that opens search. The Skills button is gone, and the Privacy policy and Terms links now sit at the bottom of Settings.
  - **Help center.** New pages on [using Proxi](/using-proxi), [how Proxi is tested](/how-proxi-is-tested) and [how Proxi's privacy compares](/privacy#how-proxi-compares) with other AI apps.
</Update>

<Update label="October 28, 2026" description="Sharing, automatic deletion and web search">
  - **Share a chat.** Turn on a public, read-only link for any chat, and turn it off again at any time.
  - **Chats delete themselves.** Chats are deleted after 30 days without activity, or after 7 or 90 days if you choose that in Settings.
  - **Theme.** Choose to match your device, or always use light or dark, in Settings.
  - **Web search.** Proxi can search the web when an answer depends on current information, and cites the pages it used.
  - **See what Proxi did.** Each reply shows a short timeline of the steps it took, like searching the help center or the web.
  - **Temporary chats.** Start a chat that isn't saved or shown in your history.
  - **Cleaner new chat screen,** with suggestions above a message box that grows as you type.
</Update>

<Update label="October 27, 2026" description="Chat titles, card masking and legal pages">
  - **Chat titles.** New chats get a short title based on your first message.
  - **Card numbers masked.** Card numbers are shown as •••• •••• •••• 1234 in your own messages, as well as before they reach the AI.
  - **Privacy policy and terms of service,** linked from the app and the help center.
  - **Help center links.** Open the help center from your profile menu, and get back to Proxi from the help center.
</Update>
