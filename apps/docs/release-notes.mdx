<Update label="November 5, 2026">
  **Faster replies and numbered citations, plus two small fixes**

  - **Faster replies.** Replies now appear more than twice as fast.
  - **Numbered citations.** Sources in a reply show as numbers that match the Sources list, and appear as soon as the text they belong to, not when the reply finishes.
  - **Buttons wait for the reply.** Copy, rating and Regenerate appear once a reply has finished, not while it's still appearing.
  - **Not now leaves a note.** Choosing Not now on a Message Lisandro card now leaves a short note in its place, and in saved chats the card no longer comes back when you reload.
</Update>

<Update label="November 4, 2026">
  **Approve memories before they're saved, smoother replies, and simpler Settings**

  - **You approve every memory.** When Proxi wants to remember something, it shows the exact text in a card under its reply. Choose Save to keep it, or Not now to skip it. See [Memory](/settings/memory).
  - **Edit before saving.** You can change the text in the card before you save it.
  - **Moving on means no.** If you send another message without choosing Save, nothing is saved.
  - **Smoother replies.** Replies now fade in word by word at an even speed, including answers based on a help center or web search, which used to show up in large blocks.
  - **Source icons show up.** Sources now show each site's icon instead of only a letter, and your browser no longer contacts an outside icon service to get them.
  - **Close button on the right.** On a phone, the X that closes Settings is now at the top right.
  - **Tidier layout.** Theme, Delete chats after and Delete all chats are now together in one list.
  - **Clearer delete buttons.** Delete account is now the only red button in Settings.
</Update>

<Update label="November 3, 2026">
  **Talk to a person, a close button for Settings, and delete all chats**

  - **Message us from the chat.** Ask to talk to a person and Proxi writes a short summary of what you need. Add your email in the card and choose Send, and we'll reply by email. See [Talk to a person](/tools/talk-to-a-person).
  - **Settings has a close button.** Settings now opens on its own screen. Close it with the X to go back to your chat.
  - **Delete all chats.** In Settings, you can now delete every chat in one step. Shared links stop working, and your memories and settings are kept.
  - **Loading spinners.** The Privacy page shows its tabs right away, and the Privacy page, Settings and your chat list show a spinner while they load. If Settings can't load, it says so with a Try again button.
  - Replies that use web search no longer show their sources twice.
</Update>

<Update label="November 2, 2026">
  **Long chats keep their context**

  - **Longer memory in long chats.** In a long chat, Proxi now keeps a short summary of the earliest messages instead of forgetting them.
  - **Clearer answers about redaction.** If you tell Proxi a card number or IBAN wasn't removed, it now explains that only valid numbers are recognized.
  - **New Privacy page.** See how many personal details Proxi removed from your messages, this month and all time, by type. Open it from the menu, under New chat.
</Update>

<Update label="November 1, 2026">
  **Clearer errors, message and chat limits, web search fixed, and account deletion**

  - **Web search fixed.** Replies that used a web search failed with "That message could not be sent". They now finish and cite the pages found.
  - **Clearer error messages.** When a reply fails, Proxi says what happened and whose problem it is, with a **Try again** button that sends your message again. See [If something goes wrong](/chats/send-messages#if-something-goes-wrong).
  - **Message length limit.** The message box stops at 20,000 characters, and tells you when you reach it.
  - **Chat limit.** A chat holds up to 100 of your messages. Proxi tells you before you send another, so you can start a new chat.
  - **Very long replies.** A chat no longer stops working after one of Proxi's replies is very long.
  - **Delete your account.** In Settings, choose Delete under Account to remove your account, chats, memories and settings.
  - **Deleted chats leave monitoring too.** When you delete a chat, or it is deleted automatically, its monitoring trace is deleted as well.
</Update>

<Update label="October 30, 2026">
  **Settings in the profile menu, search and sidebar polish, and source icons**

  - **Settings moved.** Open Settings from your profile menu at the bottom of the sidebar. The gear button next to it is gone.
  - **Simpler profile menu.** The menu now has Settings, Help Center and Logout. The Privacy and Security shortcuts are removed; both pages are still in the [help center](/privacy).
  - **Fewer repeated source links.** When several lines in a row come from the same page, its source link appears once instead of after every line.
  - **Search is ready to type.** Opening search puts the cursor in the field straight away.
  - **Search groups chats by date.** Chats are listed under Today, Yesterday, Previous 7 days and then by month, instead of a date on every row.
  - **Closer chats in the sidebar.** Less space between chats, so more fit on screen.
  - **Even spacing in chats.** Your messages and Proxi's replies now have the same space between them. On phones, Edit and Copy always show under your messages.
  - **Sidebar header stays put.** The logo and search stay at the top while you scroll your chats.
  - **Sources look like search results.** Each source shows the site's icon, its address and a larger title.
  - **Easier to read in dark mode.** The thinking label uses a lighter purple.
</Update>

<Update label="October 29, 2026">
  **Incognito chats, memory, custom instructions, search, Auto model and a shorter sidebar**

  - **Incognito chats.** Temporary chats are now called incognito chats, with a new icon and a link to what they keep out.
  - **Memory.** Ask Proxi to remember something and it will use it in future chats. Saved memories are listed in Settings, where you can delete them.
  - **Custom instructions.** Tell Proxi about yourself and how you'd like it to respond, in Settings.
  - **Search your chats.** A search field at the top of the sidebar, or Ctrl K (⌘K on a Mac), finds chats by title.
  - **Steadier sidebar button.** The button that opens and closes the sidebar now stays in the same place.
  - **Privacy policy and terms** moved from the account menu to Settings.
  - **Auto model.** Auto is the new default: it picks Haiku, Sonnet or Opus for each message, depending on how hard it is. You can still choose a model yourself. See [Choose a model](/chats/models).
  - **Calmer look.** Incognito chats and the model menu use quieter colors, and Settings is simpler.
  - **A shorter sidebar.** Your 10 most recent chats are listed under Recents, with a **View all** link that opens search. The Skills button is gone, and the Privacy policy and Terms links now sit at the bottom of Settings.
  - **Help center.** New pages on [using Proxi](/chats/send-messages), [how Proxi is tested](/how-proxi-is-tested) and [how Proxi's privacy compares](/privacy#how-proxi-compares) with other AI apps.
</Update>

<Update label="October 28, 2026">
  **Sharing, automatic deletion and web search**

  - **Share a chat.** Turn on a public, read-only link for any chat, and turn it off again at any time.
  - **Chats delete themselves.** Chats are deleted after 30 days without activity, or after 7 or 90 days if you choose that in Settings.
  - **Theme.** Choose to match your device, or always use light or dark, in Settings.
  - **Web search.** Proxi can search the web when an answer depends on current information, and cites the pages it used.
  - **See what Proxi did.** Each reply shows a short timeline of the steps it took, like searching the help center or the web.
  - **Temporary chats.** Start a chat that isn't saved or shown in your history.
  - **Cleaner new chat screen,** with suggestions above a message box that grows as you type.
</Update>

<Update label="October 27, 2026">
  **Chat titles, card masking and legal pages**

  - **Chat titles.** New chats get a short title based on your first message.
  - **Card numbers masked.** Card numbers are shown as •••• •••• •••• 1234 in your own messages, as well as before they reach the AI.
  - **Privacy policy and terms of service,** linked from the app and the help center.
  - **Help center links.** Open the help center from your profile menu, and get back to Proxi from the help center.
</Update>
