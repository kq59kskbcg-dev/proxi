---
title: "Incognito chats"
description: "Chats that aren't saved, traced, remembered or used for training."
---

Turn on incognito with the dashed chat bubble at the top right of a new chat.

## What incognito does

- Isn't saved to your history, so it isn't listed or searchable.
- Isn't traced for monitoring.
- Doesn't save to [memory](/settings/memory) and doesn't use your saved memories.
- Isn't used to train AI models.
- Disappears when you reload the page.
- Can't be [shared](/chats/share).

[Custom instructions](/settings/custom-instructions) still apply to incognito chats.

## Limits

The reply is written by Claude through Anthropic's API, so Anthropic's own terms apply to that request.
