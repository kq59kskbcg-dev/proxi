---
title: "Send messages"
description: "Send a message, stop, edit, regenerate, copy and rate replies, plus limits and what to do when a reply fails."
---

Type in the message box at the bottom of a chat and send it. Proxi replies in the language you write in. Each reply shows a short list of the steps it took, such as searching the help center or the web.

## Suggested replies

When a reply ends with a question you can answer in a word or two, such as "Should I send it?" or "Which do you prefer, a short example or step by step?", Proxi shows a card above the message box with 2 or 3 suggested answers and a short title that sums up the question. Tap a row to send it, or type your own answer in the box below. The card goes away when you start typing. Suggestions are written by a separate, smaller model that reads only the end of Proxi's reply, not your messages.

## Stop a reply

While Proxi is writing, use **Stop generating** to end the reply early.

## Edit a message

Use **Edit message** under one of your messages. On a computer it appears when you hover the message; on a phone it's always shown. The message, and everything after it, is removed from the chat and put back in the message box with its attachments. Change it and send it again.

## Regenerate a reply

Use **Regenerate** under a reply to replace that reply with a new answer to the same message. Replies can differ each time.

## Copy

Use **Copy message** or **Copy response** to copy the text to your clipboard.

## Rate a reply

Use the thumbs up or thumbs down under a reply to tell us whether it helped. Your rating is saved with the reply and used to find answers that need improving. Ratings aren't saved for [incognito chats](/chats/incognito), because those aren't traced.

## Limits

- A message can be up to 20,000 characters. The message box stops there, and tells you if you paste more.
- A chat can hold up to 100 of your messages, each with Proxi's reply. When it's full, Proxi tells you before you send another, and you can start a new chat to keep going.
- Proxi writes each reply from the most recent part of the chat: about your last 20 messages and its replies. Earlier messages are boiled down to a short summary, so Proxi keeps the main points of a long chat, but not every detail.

## If something goes wrong

When a reply fails, Proxi shows a message under your question with a **Try again** button. It says whose problem it is, so you know when your message was fine. **Try again** sends your last message again and replaces the failed reply. Anything Proxi had already written stays visible until you try again.

| Message | What it means |
|---|---|
| Proxi is busy right now | Too many requests at once. Try again in a minute. |
| Too many messages | You sent a lot of messages in a short time. Wait a few minutes. |
| Your session expired | **Try again** signs you back in. If it keeps happening, reload the page. |
| Couldn't reach Proxi | Your connection dropped. Check it, then try again. |
| Something went wrong on our side | Your message is fine. Try again. |

Each message ends with a short reference, such as "Ref 400". Quote it if you report a problem.
