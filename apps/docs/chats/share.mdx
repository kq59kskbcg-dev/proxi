---
title: "Share a chat"
description: "Turn on a public, read-only link for a chat, and turn it off again."
---

Use **Share** at the top of a chat to turn on a public, read-only link, then **Copy link**.

## What people see

- Anyone with the link can read the chat, as Proxi saw it: redacted, with no name or account details.
- Attachments show only as "PDF attached".
- New messages in the chat also become visible through the link.

## Stop sharing

Choose **Stop sharing** in the Share menu or in **Settings**, or [delete the chat](/history/delete-chats). The link stops working straight away.

## See all your shared links

Settings lists every chat you're sharing, under **Shared links**. Each one shows when its link expires. Use the copy button to copy a link again, or the stop sharing button to turn it off. Choose a chat's title to open it.

## When links expire

A link stops working on the date shown in Settings, when its chat is due for [automatic deletion](/history/delete-chats#automatic-deletion), and it leaves the list. That date comes from your **Delete chats after** setting, counted from the chat's last message, so it moves later when you keep chatting in it.

## Search engines

Shared chats are kept out of search engines.

## Incognito chats

[Incognito chats](/chats/incognito) can't be shared.
