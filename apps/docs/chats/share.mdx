---
title: "Share a chat"
description: "Turn on a public, read-only link for a chat, and turn it off again."
---

Use **Share** at the top of a chat to turn on a public, read-only link, then **Copy link**.

## What people see

- Anyone with the link can read the chat, as Proxi saw it: redacted, with no name or account details.
- Attachments show only as "PDF attached".
- New messages in the chat also become visible through the link.

## Stop sharing

Choose **Stop sharing** in the Share menu, or [delete the chat](/history/delete-chats). The link stops working.

## Search engines

Shared chats are kept out of search engines.

## Incognito chats

[Incognito chats](/chats/incognito) can't be shared.
